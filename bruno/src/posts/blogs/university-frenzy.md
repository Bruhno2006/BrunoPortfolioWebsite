Even with the beginning of university, everything is getting a *bit* out of hand...

I've officially started my 3rd year university journey on *Tuesday 29th September*, *2pm in the afternoon*, where I was bombarded with 3 lectures, the last being 2-hours long, going back-to-back. It started 2pm, then ended 6pm - is this how asbolute boredom feels like? But I can't really be complaining right now, I absolutely need to step up my game for this semester.

1st semester I smashed all expectations with a 70% average (2:1) But then I suddenly dropped to 50% average (2:2), barely passing into the next year. That news quite literally broke my self-esteem immensely and made me pretty depressed throughout the rest of my summer. Mind you, I aced most of my assignments and courseworks so it wasn't due to my lack of skills but to me having trouble with the exams. **FUCK**!

So, with this new year and my ongoing **final year project**, I aim to get as much good marks as possible and smash through exams to get a 1:1! Or at least a 2:1! But hopefully a 1:1!

Back to my first week experience, I've moved into my new accomodation for the 3rd semester with the some of the same housemates from last year. I absolutely don't regret staying with them again - despite the poor ending of 2nd semester, they truly made my time staying in shared areas an compared to 1st semester. The house situation beforehand wasn't like 1st year Founders, thank god for that! I will never return to shared-everything and no-privacy ever again!
